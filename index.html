Three parks, 200 levels each

Back Off!不要貼貼！

These monkeys need their personal space. Exactly one monkey in every row, every column and every colored turf, and no two monkeys may touch, not even diagonally.

  • Tap once for a banana ("no monkey here"), again for a monkey
  • The first 3 levels place helper bananas for you
  • In level 1, the Old Monkey teaches you step by step

Hangry!香蕉外送中

A hungry monkey is a grumpy monkey. Drag a vine from each monkey to the banana of the same color. Vines can't cross, and every square must be filled.

  • Monkeys who haven't reached their banana get mad
  • Fill every square to clear the level

Outta My Way!借過借過！

Eek is pushing a banana cart home, and sleepy, sprawling monkeys are lying across the road. Drag them aside (each one only slides the way it's lying) and roll the cart out the exit.

  • Poke a sleepy monkey and it wakes up with a grumpy "huh?"
  • Gray rocks don't budge
  • The screen shows the fewest moves possible
More parks are on the way. Squeak!

Stuck? Ask the Old Monkey

The Old Monkey doesn't just hand you the answer. He tells you why, like "this turf has only one square left", so you learn the trick instead of just getting past the level.

Squeaks, tunes and sass

  • 13 monkey expressions, each with its own squeak
  • Every park has its own sound effects and music; the lobby plays laid-back lo-fi
  • All artwork, sounds and music are original

三座樂園，各 200 關

不要貼貼！Back Off!

這群猴子非常需要個人空間。每一行、每一列、每一塊彩色地盤都要剛好一隻猴子，而且猴子不能相鄰，斜角也不行。

  • 點一下放香蕉（代表這格沒猴子），再點一下放猴子
  • 前 3 關自動幫你放香蕉
  • 第 1 關有老猴子一步一步教

香蕉外送中Hangry!

猴子一餓，脾氣就來。從猴子拖出藤蔓連到同色香蕉，藤蔓不能交叉，還要鋪滿每一格。

  • 還沒吃到香蕉的猴子會一直生氣
  • 每一格都要鋪滿才算過關

借過借過！Outta My Way!

吱吱推著香蕉推車要回家，路上卻躺滿了睡死猴和路霸猴。把牠們拖開（只能沿著自己躺的方向滑），把推車推出出口。

  • 戳醒睡死猴會被「嗯？」一聲
  • 灰色的石頭推不動
  • 畫面會顯示最少要幾步
更多樂園陸續推出．吱吱！

卡關？問問老猴子

老猴子不會直接把答案丟給你，而是告訴你為什麼，例如「這塊地盤只剩一格」。學會這一招，下一關就不會再卡。

吱吱叫、配樂和欠扁表情

  • 13 種猴子表情，每一種都有自己的吱吱叫聲
  • 每座樂園有自己的音效與背景音樂，大廳是放鬆的 lo-fi 音樂
  • 所有圖案、音效、音樂都是原創
