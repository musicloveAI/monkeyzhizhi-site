Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-27

Sassy Monkeys collects no data.
No tracking, no ads, no third-party SDKs, no account, no servers. Your game progress stays on your device.

This policy explains how Sassy Monkeys (猴子吱吱, "the App") handles your data. The App is made by musicloveAI, an independent developer ("we").

1. We collect no personal data

The App does not collect, store on our side, or transmit any personal or usage data, including but not limited to: name, email, phone number, device identifiers, the advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, location, contacts, photos, usage behavior, game history, crash logs and diagnostics. The App's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

2. No tracking

The App does not track you across apps or websites and shares nothing with data brokers, so it never asks for tracking permission.

3. No ads, no third-party SDKs

The App shows no ads and contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, social-login or any other third-party SDKs.

4. No account, no servers

You never sign up or log in. We run no servers, and the App sends nothing to us.

5. Where your progress and settings live

  • Your game progress (which levels you've cleared) and settings (such as sound on/off) are stored only in the App's folder on your device. The App never uploads them to the cloud or to any server, and we cannot see them.
  • Deleting the App deletes your progress and settings, and we cannot restore them. "Settings → Reset All Progress" in the App also erases your progress.
  • The progress file is marked excluded from iCloud Backup and computer backups, so it never leaves your device in any form. Deleting the app deletes it.

6. Network use

Apart from App Store purchases and restoring purchases, the App does not use the internet. Links in Settings such as "Privacy Policy" and "Terms of Use" open this website in Safari. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, analytics, trackers or external fonts; as the host, GitHub may keep standard access logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Purchases are handled by Apple

Park unlocks and "Buy the Developer a Coffee" tips are App Store in-app purchases. Payment, receipts and refunds are handled by Apple. The App only learns, through Apple's StoreKit, whether a park has been purchased; it never receives your name, Apple Account, payment method or any payment details. For how Apple handles your data, see the Apple Privacy Policy.

8. Children's privacy

The App is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. Because the App collects no data from anyone, it collects no personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). Parents can restrict in-app purchases with iOS Screen Time.

9. When you email us

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply to you. We never use them for marketing or share them with anyone, and you can ask us to delete our correspondence at any time.

10. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. The data on your device is entirely under your control: delete the App to remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a future version ever needs to collect any data, we will say so clearly in the App beforehand and update the App Store privacy label.

12. Contact

隱私權政策

生效日：2026-09-27

猴子吱吱不收集任何資料。
不追蹤、沒有廣告、沒有第三方 SDK、沒有帳號、沒有伺服器。遊戲進度只存在你的裝置裡。

本政策說明「猴子吱吱」（英文名稱 Sassy Monkeys，以下稱「本 App」）如何處理你的資料。本 App 由個人獨立開發者 musicloveAI（以下稱「我們」）開發。

1. 我們不收集任何個人資料

本 App 不收集、不儲存在我們這端、也不傳送任何個人資料或使用資料，包括但不限於：姓名、Email、電話、裝置識別碼、廣告識別碼（IDFA）、IP 位址、位置、聯絡人、照片、使用行為、遊戲紀錄、當機紀錄與診斷資料。本 App 在 App Store 的隱私標籤為「未收集資料」。

2. 不追蹤

本 App 不會跨 App 或網站追蹤你，不與資料仲介分享任何資料，也因此不會跳出「允許追蹤」的詢問。

3. 沒有廣告，沒有第三方 SDK

本 App 沒有任何廣告，也沒有使用分析、廣告、當機回報、社群登入或任何其他第三方 SDK。

4. 沒有帳號，沒有伺服器

你不需要註冊或登入。我們沒有任何伺服器，本 App 也不會把資料傳給我們。

5. 遊戲進度與設定存在哪裡

  • 遊戲進度（過了哪些關）與設定（音效開關等）只存在你裝置上本 App 的資料夾裡。本 App 不會把它們上傳到雲端或任何伺服器，我們也看不到。
  • 刪除本 App，進度與設定會一起刪除，且無法由我們復原。App 內「設定 → 重設所有進度」也會刪除進度。
  • 遊戲進度檔已設定不納入 iCloud 備份，也不納入電腦備份，所以不會以任何形式離開你的裝置。刪除 App 就會一起刪除。

6. 網路使用

除了 App Store 的購買與恢復購買之外，本 App 不使用網路。設定裡的「隱私權政策」「使用條款」等連結會用 Safari 開啟本網站。本網站以 GitHub Pages 託管，不使用 cookie、分析、追蹤或外部字型；GitHub 作為主機商，可能為了安全與維運記錄一般的連線紀錄（例如 IP 位址），詳見 GitHub 隱私聲明。

7. 購買由 Apple 處理

樂園買斷與「請開發者喝杯咖啡」打賞都是 App Store 的 App 內購買，付款、收據與退款都由 Apple 處理。本 App 只透過 Apple 的 StoreKit 得知某座樂園是否已購買，不會取得你的姓名、Apple 帳號、付款方式或任何付款資訊。Apple 如何處理你的資料，請見 Apple 隱私權政策。

8. 兒童隱私

本 App 分級為 4+，適合所有年齡。因為本 App 不收集任何人的資料，也就不會收集 13 歲以下（或你所在地區法定年齡以下）兒童的任何個人資料。家長可以用 iOS 的「螢幕使用時間」限制 App 內購買。

9. 你寫信給我們的時候

如果你主動寄 Email 給我們，我們會收到你的 Email 地址與信件內容，只用來回覆你，不會用於行銷、不會分享給任何人；你可以隨時要求我們刪除往來信件。

10. 你的權利

因為我們沒有你的任何資料，所以沒有可以查詢、更正、匯出或刪除的資料。你的裝置上的資料完全由你掌控：刪除 App 即可清除。

11. 政策變更

如果本政策有變更，我們會更新本頁並修改生效日。若未來版本要收集任何資料，我們會事先在 App 內清楚告知，並更新 App Store 的隱私標籤。
